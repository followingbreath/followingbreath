When This Isn't a Good Fit

This work involves intentional, intense breathing, so it isn't right for everyone. Please tell me on our free call if any of the following apply to you, so we can decide together whether to proceed, adjust how we work, or choose something gentler instead:

  • Pregnancy
  • Cardiovascular conditions: high blood pressure, heart disease, aneurysm, or recent heart surgery
  • Epilepsy or other seizure disorders
  • Glaucoma, detached retina, or recent eye surgery
  • Severe asthma or other respiratory conditions
  • Recent surgery, significant physical injury, or osteoporosis
  • Psychosis, bipolar disorder, or significant unprocessed trauma, especially without a support team in place
  • Any medication affecting heart rate, blood pressure, or psychiatric state

If any of these apply, it doesn't automatically rule the work out, it just means we go more gently, adapt the approach, or check with your doctor first. If you're unsure, just ask.

The Transformation Series

In person only

10 Sessions of Rebirthing Breathwork

Rebirthing breathwork was never meant to be a one-off. From the earliest teachings of the practice, ten sessions has been the recommended minimum, giving the process enough time and trust to go beneath the surface and reach something that actually lasts. A single session can shift how you feel for a day. A series changes the pattern itself, and the real depth tends to open up a few sessions in. The aim by the end is that you can keep working with your own breath on your own, not that you need to keep coming back to me.

This is work best met in person. Being physically present together allows for closer, more responsive guidance through the breath, and a level of safety and grounding that's hard to fully recreate over a screen, especially as the sessions get more intense. For that reason, the full Transformation Series is offered in person only, at locations depending on where I'm currently based or traveling. Get in touch to check current availability near you.

Depending on what feels right for you in the moment, I'll sometimes bring in aroma, sound, or gentle bodywork alongside the breath, never assumed, only if you're comfortable with it. These aren't improvised extras: they come from training in bodywork (Thai yoga techniques, reflexology, moxa) and sound healing, so if they come into a session, they're held with the same care as the breathwork itself. You're always in control of what happens in the room.
